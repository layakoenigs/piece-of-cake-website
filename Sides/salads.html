Sweet Potato Salad
Oil dressing

Serves: 4 Course: Sides Type: Salad
Time: 20 Min Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

2 pounds sweet potatoes (3 medium or 2 large)
4 tablespoons olive oil, divided
1 tablespoon chili powder
3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, divided, plus more to taste
1/2 medium red onion
1 large lime
2 (14-ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
1 to 2 packed cups baby spinach
1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more to taste
4 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (about 1 cup)

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven.
  2. Peel the sweet potatoes, if desired, and cut into 1-inch cubes. Place on a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and sprinkle with the chili powder and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt. Toss to coat.
  3. Arrange the potatoes in a single layer. Roast, tossing halfway through, until tender and browned in spots, about 30 minutes.
  4. Once the potatoes are done, let them cool on the baking sheet until just warm to the touch, 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, slice the red onion into very thin half-moons and place in a large bowl. Finely grate the zest of the lime into the bowl, then halve the lime crosswise and squeeze its juice into the bowl. Toss to coat the onion in the juice then leave to quick-pickle while the potatoes roast.
  6. Add the roasted sweet potatoes, black beans, baby spinach, remaining 2 tablespoons olive oil, remaining 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and black pepper to the bowl with the onion and lime juice and toss to combine. Add the feta and toss gently again to combine.
  7. Taste and season with additional salt and pepper, as needed. Enjoy warm or at room temperature.

Mushroom broccoli Salad
vinaigrette

Serves: 4 Course: Sides Type: Salad
Time: 35 Min Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

3 ½ c broccoli cut into florets
½ tsp salt
2 tablespoon olive oil
3 cloves garlic
4 oz mushrooms sliced
17 olives
¼ c silvered chopped almonds

Dressing

2 tablespoon lime juice
1 tablespoon truffle oil
1 tablespoon soy sauce or Tamari
1 teaspoon Maple syrup
½ tsp salt
¼ tsp pepper
½ c red onion thinly sliced

Directions

  1. In a medium bowl whisk all ingredients for the sauce. Add thinly sliced onion and marinate for 15-30 minutes.
  2. Bring a medium pot of water to a boil. Add ½ teaspoon of salt, broccoli florets and cook for 2 minutes. Immediately drain and put broccoli in bowl filled with ice cold water. After 3 minutes drain broccoli.
  3. Heat olive oil in a medium pan and over medium heat. Add crushed garlic and cook for 40 seconds. Discard garlic and add sliced Shiitake mushrooms and cook for 5-7 minutes until mushrooms start to brown. Transfer to a plate.
  4. In a large bowl combine cooked broccoli, mushrooms, red onion along with the marinade, pitted olives and almonds. Mix well and serve.

Avocado fruit salad
sweet dressing

Serves: 4 Course: Sides Type: Salad
Time: 10 Min Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

lettuce
apple, peeled and diced
strawberries, sliced
avocado, diced
dried cranberries
sliced green onions
toasted sliced almonds
crumbled feta

Dressing

3 tablespoons fresh orange juice
3 tablespoons olive oil
2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
1 clove garlic
salt and pepper

Directions

  1. For the salad: Arrange all ingredients in a bowl. Measurements are up to you and how much you like of everything.
  2. For the dressing: Add all ingredients to a blender. Blend until smooth. Dressing can be made ahead of time and stored in the fridge. Toss salad with dressing right before serving to avoid sogginess.

Sushi salad
sweet sauce and spicy mayo

Serves: 6-8 Course: Sides Type: Salad
Time: 40 Min Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

2 cups sushi rice
3 cups water
3-4 tbsp rice vinegar
1 tbsp sugar
1/2-1 tsp salt

SALMON

1-1.5 lb salmon
1 tbsp honey
Pinch of salt

TOPPINGS

2 avocado, sliced
2 large carrots, peeled and diced
3 persian cucumbers, thinly sliced or cubed
1 sheet nori, cut into pieces
1/4 cup store bought crispy fried onions
Sesame seeds
Sweet sauce and spicy mayo, drizzled on top

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 F. Marinate the salmon in the coconut aminos, honey, and salt and set aside and bake for 15-18 minutes. Remove from oven and flake with a fork, discarding the skin.
  2. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear. Soak the sushi rice for 30 minutes- if running short on time, you can skip the soaking step.
  3. While the rice soaks, start dicing your veggies and preparing the sauces.
  4. Drain the rice and add to a pot with the water. Cover and bring to a boil. Once boiling, reduce heat and let is simmer for about 20 minutes.
  5. Once the rice is finished, remove from heat and let it sit for 10 minutes, then season with the rice vinegar, sugar and salt.
  6. On a large platter, layer your sushi rice and top with the crumbled seaweed, flaked salmon, cucumbers, carrots, avocado slices, and fried onions. Finish it off with a drizzle of the sauces, some sesame seeds and furikake if using.
  7. I normally serve this at room temperature. If preparing ahead of time, you can prepare the ingredients and assemble everything before serving. Or, you can assemble everything except for the fried onions and sauces and add those right before serving.

Tips on presentation
Build Vertically:
Instead of spreading greens flat, mound them high in the center of the plate to create volume and visual energy.		 Showcase Ingredients:
Don't bury the best parts; place colorful toppings like protein, nuts, or berries on top of the greens so they are immediately visible.
Vary the Cuts:
Use a mandolin or peeler to create different shapes, such as cucumber ribbons, shaved radish discs, or julienned carrots. 		Use High-Contrast Colors:
Pair vibrant opposites, such as purple radicchio with bright orange citrus or dark kale with white feta cheese.
Drizzle, Don't Drown:
Apply dressing in a light, decorative stream or serve it on the side to prevent the greens from wilting and looking soggy. 		Frame with Negative Space:
Use a large, clean plate (preferably white) and leave a clear border around the edges to keep the presentation focused and elegant.
