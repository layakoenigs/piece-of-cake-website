Kugel

Discover the ultimate comfort food with this recipe for kugel, a classic Jewish dish that bakes into a fluffy, golden, and satisfying pudding.

Cherry apple kugel
With oatmeal

Serves: 12 Course: Sides Type: Kugel
Time: 1 hour and 20 Mins Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

2 cups ﬂour
2 cups oats
2 teaspoons cinnamon
2 sticks margarine
1 and 1/3 cups brown sugar

FILLING

3 green apples, peeled and sliced thinly
1 (20-ounce) can Cherry Pie Filling

Directions

  1. Mix ﬂour, oats, and cinnamon in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Melt margarine and brown sugar together in the microwave and add to the ﬂour-oat mixture.
  3. Press half of this “dough” in an even layer into the bottom of a nine- by 13-inch pan.
  4. Preheat oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit.
  5. Layer sliced apples over the “dough.”
  6. Pour cherry pie ﬁlling over apples.
  7. Crumble remaining “dough” mixture on top.
  8. Bake, uncovered, at 350 degrees for 45 minutes.

Potato kugel
A must make

Serves: 9 Course: Sides Type: Kugel
Time: 1 hour and 15 Mins Level: Medium Family

Ingredients

1 1/2 lbs Russet potatoes, baking, about 3 medium
2 medium yellow onions, peeled and cut into chunks
3 large eggs, well beaten
1 1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp pepper
1/3 cup matzah meal
3 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Directions

  1. First, preheat the oven to 350 degrees and place an 8" x 8" baking dish inside to get nice and hot while the oven is preheating.
  2. Peel the potatoes and drop them into a bowl of cool water to prevent them from turning brown while you’re working. Attach the grater wheel to a food processor and shred the potatoes, working as quickly as possible. Once all of the potatoes are grated, remove them from the food processor and place them in a colander to drain.
  3. Next, remove the grater attachment from the food processor and switch to the steel blade. Add the onions (and any pieces of potato that did not shred properly) into the food processor and pulse until very small, but not liquified. Transfer the onions to a large mixing bowl then set aside.
  4. Cover the shredded potatoes with a paper towel, pressing down firmly with your hands to extract as much liquid from them as possible. Allow any of the liquid that is removed from the potatoes to drain away.
  5. Once you have removed as much liquid as possible from the potatoes, remove the paper towel. Add the potatoes to the bowl containing the onions and mix well—once the onions and the potatoes have been combined, you no longer have to worry about the potatoes turning brown so quickly.
  6. Add the eggs, salt, pepper and matzah meal to the mixture and stir until completely incorporated.
  7. Once the oven is preheated, remove the hot casserole dish from the oven and add 2 tbsp of olive oil to the bottom of the baking dish. Carefully tip the dish to allow the oil to coat the bottom.
  8. Slowly and carefully pour in the kugel mixture, spreading evenly.
  9. Drizzle the remaining tbsp of extra virgin olive oil over the top of the kugel and return it to the oven. Bake for 45−55 minutes or until deeply brown and crispy on the top and edges.
  10. Remove from the oven, allow the kugel to rest for 10−15 minutes then serve warm. Enjoy!

Pumpkin Pie
Sesame Caramel Topping

Serves: 10 Course: Sides Type: Kugel
Time: 30 Min Level: Medium Family

Ingredients

3/4 cup sugar
2 tbsp corn syrup
2 tbsp tahini
4 tbsp unsalted butter
1/4 cup heavy cream
1/4 cup sesame seeds
1/2 tsp kosher salt
1 (22 oz) 8-inch bakery pumpkin pie

Directions

  1. In a medium saucepan, heat the sugar, corn syrup, and 2 tbsp water to a boil on medium-high, stirring with a rubber spatula to dissolve sugar.
  2. Once sugar is dissolved, cook without stirring 5–7 min., until sugar caramelizes and turns golden brown.
  3. As soon as caramel is an amber color, remove saucepan from heat.
  4. Carefully stir the tahini and butter into caramel.
  5. Add the cream, sesame seeds, and salt. Whisk to combine.
  6. Let cool 1 hour or until room temperature.
  7. Spoon caramel over the pumpkin pie. Refrigerate 2–3 hours, until caramel has thickened and is just set.

Broccoli Kugel

Serves: 8 Course: Sides Type: Kugel
Time: 1 hour Level: Easy Family

Ingredients

3 Tbsp flour
3 Tbsp onion soup mix
3 Tbsp mayo
1 bag of frozen broccoli
3 eggs

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Put in a greased pan. Bake for 45 min at 350.

Tips on cutting Vegetables
Use the "Claw Grip":
Curl your fingertips inward and use your knuckles to guide the blade. This keeps your fingers safe and away from the sharp edge.		 Stabilize Your Board:
Place a damp paper towel or non-slip mat under your cutting board to prevent it from sliding during use.
Create a Flat Base:
Slice a thin sliver off round vegetables (like onions or potatoes) to create a flat surface. This prevents them from rolling while you cut. 		Maintain a Sharp Knife:
Use a sharp Chef’s knife to reduce the force needed to slice. If you need a refresh, the KitchenAid Knife Sharpening Guide covers basic maintenance.
Cut for Uniformity:
Aim for consistent sizes (like dicing or julienning) so all pieces cook at the same rate, preventing some from being mushy while others are raw. 		Leave the Root Intact:
When dicing onions or shallots, leave the root end attached. It acts as a handle that holds the layers together as you slice.
