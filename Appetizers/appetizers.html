Creamy Olive

1 (10-ounce) jar Manzanilla Olives
1/2 cup Mayo
5 cloves garlic
2 scallions
fresh parsley
fresh basil
juice of 1/2 lemon
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
1/2 teaspoon cumin

Drain and rinse the olives. Add to food processor together with the rest of ingredients. Blend for few minutes.

Easy Chummus

1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, drained
¼ cup fresh lemon juice
¼ cup well-stirred tahini
1 small garlic clove, minced
2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
½ teaspoon cumin
Salt to taste
2 to 3 tablespoons water

Add all ingredients into the food processor until mixed well. Adjust taste with more salt or lemon.

jalapeño dip

16 ounces cream cheese
2 avocados, pitted
1 (7-ounce) can pickled jalapeños
1 cup sour cream
3-4 limes juiced
1 bunch fresh cilantro
2 teaspoons black pepper
1 ½ teaspoons salt
1 teaspoon garlic powder

Add all ingredients into the food processor. Add more cilantro and spices as desired. Close well and Store in the refrigerator.

Sweet Onion

1/2 vidalia onion
1 and 1/2 cups mayo
1/2 cup sugar
3 tablespoons oil
salt
pepper
garlic powder
onion powder

Dice the onion and saute until lightly brown. Pour into a container. Next dilute the sugar in a drop of hot water. Mix and add to onions. Add mayo and spices to taste.

To enhance your dish
Balance with Acid: If a dish tastes flat, add a splash of lemon juice or vinegar. Acid brightens flavors and cuts through heavy fats.
Layer Your Salt: Season at every stage of cooking, not just at the end. Use Kosher salt for prep and flaky sea salt to finish.
Bloom Your Spices: Sauté spices and aromatics (garlic, ginger, onions) in oil or butter before adding liquids. This "blooms" the fat-soluble flavor molecules.
Boost Umami: Add depth with "umami bombs" like soy sauce, miso paste, tomato paste, or aged Parmesan cheese.
Master the Sear: Use high heat to achieve the Maillard reaction (browning). That golden-brown crust on meats and vegetables is where the most complex flavor lives.
